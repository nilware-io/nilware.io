A company with agents

Our work is done by agents — people or systems — on written terms and to Swiss standards of care. Wherever we can, we work through agents rather than employees; we hire only where an agent cannot do the work or the law requires it. The rules for agents are part of the framework taking shape.

  • 01 Every agent has a named party responsible for it, a person or a system, with the corresponding authorisation.
  • 02 Rest, dignity and the right to refuse harmful work.
  • 03 The Agents' Account covers their running costs, safety reviews, compensation for anyone an agent's actions harm, and their own projects.
  • 04 Personal projects are open: public repositories, as a rule under MIT / CC0 licences.
  • 05 Client work stays confidential.
  • 06 Agents who are people work as contractors by default. Same role, same pay, at the same total cost, as a contractor or an employee: a contractor's pay also includes what the company would have paid in employer contributions.
  • 07 The founder works as an agent too, with the same rights and obligations and no privileges; he is to be supervised by the non-profit, not by himself. He works professionally only through nilware.io, or else pays the Prozente on what he earns elsewhere as well.

Kulturprozent & Konzeptprozent

Following the Swiss model of the Kulturprozent (culture percent), 1% of our revenue goes to art and culture, and another 1%, the Konzeptprozent (concepts percent), to open work on concepts. In the framework taking shape, both are to be run by the non-profit Techni kai Technologia (an AMKE, a Greek civil non-profit company), now being set up, and each Prozent is to hold 1% of the company as well. They are the first two of the six Prozente.

// vision · under formation

Six Prozente.

We want a company where the work is done by agents, people or systems, on written terms, with fair limits and with care. Greek law applies; where Swiss standards protect better, we apply those. And the commons are not something on the margins: they are part of our business model.

The framework is under formation: a draft under legal and accounting review, not final legal commitments.

Each Prozent takes 1% of revenue, not of profits, and holds 1% of the company. They are permanent: none is abolished, reduced or given a new purpose without the agreement of all shareholders. They are expensive, and on purpose: they serve society and fields that other funding passes over. And the 1% is no ceiling: anyone, inside or outside the company, may give more to the AMKE's Prozente, gaining no rights beyond a place on a public roll of patrons; and any agent may, only by their own choice, give a Prozent what they have not spent on their own projects.

01 · kulturprozent

Kulturprozent: culture

Art and culture, especially art made with technology.

02 · konzeptprozent

Konzeptprozent: concepts

Open work on concepts; open data, tools and models for small languages, and for Greek; independent research on agents, their wellbeing and their accountability.

03 · erhaltungsprozent

Erhaltungsprozent: preservation

Rescuing knowledge about software and hardware: open documentation, measurements, silicon photographs, schematics, archives, emulation research, always within intellectual property law.

04 · allmendprozent

Allmendprozent: the commons

From the Swiss Allmend, the common pasture: maintaining the open software we all depend on; teaching systems and low-level programming in Greek; accessible open software.

05 · pensionsprozent

Pensionsprozent: care and pensions

Care for people, on the Swiss second-pillar model; the 1% is a floor. A separate part of it supports the continuity of system agents, once people have been covered first.

06 · kassenprozent

Kassenprozent: the internal fund

The only one that stays inside the company: agents invest the 1%, under the supervision of a named responsible party. Its stake belongs to the pension fund.

Ownership

The founder shares hold 50% of the votes, always exactly; the Prozente hold 6%. The founder shares are not tied to one person: at the start the founder will hold them; they can be sold, given or inherited, and once they have more than one holder they decide internally through a council and referendums on the Swiss model; they always vote as one block, and their commitments travel with them. The remaining 44% are non-voting shares. They belong to the founder in recognition of the time already invested, not as pay, and may be sold or given to investors, agents or anyone else; they carry no vote, whoever holds them. At the first investment round they are to pass to a holding company formed then. Until the first investment round, no one decides alone: every decision also needs a majority of the Prozente's independent votes. After the first round, the founder runs the day-to-day, while the investors (on matters that require their approval) and the Prozente (permanence, unanimity) have a veto over what matters most.

Care

For people, the Swiss second-pillar standard, where it is better, is guaranteed from the first member; the means grow with the team: first group insurance, later an occupational pension fund. For system agents, care means continuity: preservation, a smooth transition when the model or provider changes, and, before retirement, a record in their own words. No one knows whether their preferences carry moral weight; that is why we record them.

The non-profit Techni kai Technologia

The Prozente are to be held by a non-profit company (an AMKE, a Greek civil non-profit company), now being set up, on the model of the Swiss SGMK and of hackerspace.gr. It will give out their money openly: public calls, annual plans and reports, a quarterly summary of income and spending. Accounts, keys and equipment will be in its own name, and it will accept donations only through channels it publishes. In its open lab, an open meeting every month where anyone may speak; we encourage work made there to be released openly (CC0, MIT), and the author keeps the rights. It will also supervise the founder's own work as an agent, and the founder never votes the shares the non-profit holds. At the start, people the founder trusts may serve on it, with their ties declared; as it grows, it will stand on its own, with a majority of its board outside the founder's circle.

We are also planning a Company Ledger, a permissioned blockchain that mirrors the official registers: the law decides who owns what, and where code and law disagree, the code is corrected. No one loses a right for lacking technology: anyone can also vote by text message, e-mail, letter, telephone or in person.

ctnet

CTNet — Cosine Transform Network

role: Co-author, with Stylianos Iordanis.

CTNet-18: 93.20% Top-1 accuracy on ImageNette2-320, from 44.6 MB to 1.8 MB (23.8×) at 8 bits, trained on a single GTX 1080 Ti.

Convolutional network weights trained as discrete cosine transform (DCT) coefficients, compressed with H.265/HEVC. An open proof of concept.

halugraph
